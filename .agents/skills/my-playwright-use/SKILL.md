# Playwright Instruction

- When editing code, capture screenshots of before and after change and store at
  the root of the workdir
- Capture only the affect area if the change is small and not spread through
  entire page
- Open browser and Playwright console in their own empty non-special Hyprland
  workspace
- Save output steps at the root of the workdir
